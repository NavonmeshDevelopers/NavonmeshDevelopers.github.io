Privacy Policy

Your Data Stays With You.

Utilix is built around a simple promise: your data stays on your device. Below is a short summary of how we handle information when you visit this website and use the Utilix application.

Information We Collect

This website does not require you to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from visitors of this informational site. The Utilix application is designed to work fully offline and does not transmit your data to external servers.

How We Use Information

Any information processed by Utilix utilities, such as clipboard content, generated passwords, screenshots, or scanned text, is handled locally on your device and is not shared, sold, or transmitted to third parties.

Third-Party Services

The website may use minimal, anonymous analytics or hosting services provided by the platform. The Utilix application itself does not integrate third-party tracking, ads, or telemetry of its own.

Your Rights & Contact

You may request access, correction, or deletion of any personal information you have shared with us by contacting Navonmesh Developers through the official website.
