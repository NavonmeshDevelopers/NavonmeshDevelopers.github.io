About Our Company

Building and Publishing Our Own Apps

Navonmesh Developers is a startup focused on creating, launching, and improving self-made apps for real everyday needs.

Our Story

Navonmesh Developers began as a builder-first startup with a simple belief: great apps should be practical, secure, and easy to use.

We design, develop, test, publish, and continuously improve our own products with a long-term product mindset.

Mission

To build self-made apps that solve real user problems through thoughtful design, dependable performance, and continuous updates.

Vision

To become a trusted app startup known for useful products, consistent quality, and user-first innovation.

Secure Vault

Your privacy, fortified.

SecureVault protects photos, videos, audio, and documents with offline-first, military-grade encryption. No cloud and no tracking - your data stays local, locked inside your personal vault.

Download SecureVault Explore features

Zero-knowledge privacy

Files are encrypted and stored locally, never uploaded to the cloud.

Industry-standard crypto

AES encryption with PBKDF2HMAC (SHA-256) key derivation.

Offline-first

Secure access even without an internet connection.

SecureVault desktop

A local digital safe for Windows 10 and 11 that encrypts, organizes, and lets you view private media and documents without exposing them.

Multi-factor local access

Secure your vault with both a password and a unique pass code.

Smart categorization

Files are organized into Images, Videos, Audio, and Documents.

Instant encryption

Drag and drop files to encrypt them and safely remove originals.

Seamless viewing

View encrypted files in-app with temporary in-memory decryption.

Why people choose SecureVault

Easy restoration

Restore decrypted files back to your PC with one click.

Credential control

Update your password and passcode from the dashboard.

Local, private storage

Your encrypted files live only on your device in secure_vault_data.

Modern interface

A sleek cyber-dark dashboard built for comfort and clarity.
